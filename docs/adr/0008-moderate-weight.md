title: Moderate process weight
status: accepted

---
## Context
Too little process loses decisions; too much stalls a one-person operation.

## Decision
RFCs only for multi-milestone or multi-decision changes; ADRs for single decisions; a review and process notes at each milestone close. No phase gates beyond milestones, no estimates or velocity tracking.

## Consequences
Retrospection is a section of the close-out PR, not a separate meeting or document.

## Alternatives
Minimal (no RFCs or reviews). Formal (phase gates, regular reports).
